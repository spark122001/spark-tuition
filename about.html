About Spark

Ropar's most trusted tuition center for Mathematics & Science since 2014.

Our Story

Spark Tuition Center was founded in 2014 by a group of passionate educators who believed every student deserved quality coaching — not just those in big cities. Located in the heart of Ropar, Punjab, we started with just 12 students and a dream.

Today, we have grown into a center of academic excellence, coaching 500+ students annually from Class 6 to 12 in Mathematics and Science. Our students regularly top CBSE and PSEB board exams and go on to prestigious engineering and medical colleges.
